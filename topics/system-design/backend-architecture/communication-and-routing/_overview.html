Communication & Routing

ELI5 big picture. A restaurant can announce that an order is ready, greet every customer at one reception desk, or provide a specialist guide for a particular group. Announcements, entry routing, and tailored assistance are different interaction patterns. The three lessons here make those responsibilities explicit.

Communication design controls who asks whom to act, what a reply or event means, and where client-facing coordination belongs. A fact notification, a gateway route, and a frontend-specific composition can coexist, but each needs an owner and a truthful failure contract.

Why this branch exists. Reducing the number of calls visible to a client does not automatically reduce total work or uncertainty. Trace downstream fan-out, duplicate effects, pending states, and the exact authority behind each result.
