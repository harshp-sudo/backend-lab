Observability & Telemetry Pillars

A delivery depot needs event notes, population-wide scoreboards and the route of a delayed parcel. A courier for those signals is different from the filing cabinet and screen that store and show them. Choose evidence for the question, then follow how it is collected and what can be lost.

Centralized Logging follows events and retention. Metrics & Monitoring applies RED/USE, objectives and time-series pipelines. Distributed Tracing explains causal request paths, while OpenTelemetry defines instrumentation and collection/export responsibilities.

Explore this branch

  • Centralized Logging

    Structured event collection, agent/collector transport, buffering, indexing/querying, correlation, clock/order ambiguity, retention and redaction; overload, duplicates/loss and required-audit boundaries.

  • Metrics & Monitoring

    RED/USE methods, time-series types/labels, SLIs/SLOs and error budgets; collection/export, Prometheus scrape/storage/query/rules and Grafana visualization, distribution aggregation and pipeline health. Mandatory Performance Monitoring cross-link.

  • Distributed Tracing

    Spans, causal context, asynchronous parent/link semantics, propagation, sampling and critical-path/wait attribution; partial traces, clock skew, sensitive attributes and diagnostic limits.

  • OpenTelemetry

    APIs/SDKs, instrumentation, context/resource/semantic conventions, OTLP and Collector receive/process/export paths; sampling, queues/retries/redaction, component maturity and backend boundaries.
