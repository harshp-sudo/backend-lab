DevOps, Deployment & Observability

Opening a new train line takes more than building a train: inspect it, put it into service gradually, watch the signals and rehearse what to do when the route closes. Software operations connects those same promises from a verified change to useful service and recoverable business state.

Follow an identifiable artifact through release, the platform that runs it, the evidence that explains its behavior and the people and backups that restore it. These four paths connect delivery decisions to outcomes rather than making tools the goal.

Explore this branch

  • CI/CD & Release Strategies

    CI/CD Pipelines tracks verified changes and artifact promotion. Blue-Green, Canary and Rolling Deployments choose different exposure and capacity strategies. All must reason about mixed versions, state compatibility and detection/recovery limits.

  • Containers, Orchestration & Infrastructure

    Docker explains images and running containers. Container Orchestration describes the general responsibilities; Kubernetes makes those control loops concrete. Infrastructure as Code manages versioned infrastructure ownership, and HPA changes desired workload replicas using measured signals.

  • Observability & Telemetry Pillars

    Centralized Logging follows events and retention. Metrics & Monitoring applies RED/USE, objectives and time-series pipelines. Distributed Tracing explains causal request paths, while OpenTelemetry defines instrumentation and collection/export responsibilities.

  • Incident Response & Business Continuity

    Alerting & Incident Management connects actionable signals, ownership, mitigation, communication and learning. Disaster Recovery & Backup Strategies connects business priorities to recoverable copies, dependencies and verified restoration.
