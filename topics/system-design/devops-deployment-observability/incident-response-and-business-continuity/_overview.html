Incident Response & Business Continuity

When a town loses power, somebody must coordinate repairs and explain the situation while others restore the services people need first. A photograph of spare batteries is not proof they work. Preparation includes clear ownership and rehearsed restoration, not just an alarm and a backup label.

Alerting & Incident Management connects actionable signals, ownership, mitigation, communication and learning. Disaster Recovery & Backup Strategies connects business priorities to recoverable copies, dependencies and verified restoration.
