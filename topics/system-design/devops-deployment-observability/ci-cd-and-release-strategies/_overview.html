CI/CD & Release Strategies

A bakery tests a recipe before serving it. It can switch between two counters, let a small group taste the new batch or replace trays gradually. Every choice needs the same ingredients and an honest way to stop; moving customers back does not un-eat a cake.

CI/CD Pipelines tracks verified changes and artifact promotion. Blue-Green, Canary and Rolling Deployments choose different exposure and capacity strategies. All must reason about mixed versions, state compatibility and detection/recovery limits.
