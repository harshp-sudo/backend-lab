Lifecycle, Eviction & Consistency

A kitchen removes an old menu when prices change, discards a note when its freshness date passes and clears shelf space when it is full. Those are different reasons to remove a copy. Even after removal, a worker carrying an old menu can put it back unless the update process accounts for the race.

This category separates invalidation, expiration, capacity eviction and the consistency a reader actually observes. Each policy needs ownership, failure assumptions and a repair path.
