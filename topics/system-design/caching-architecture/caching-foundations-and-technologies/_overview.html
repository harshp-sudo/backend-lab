Caching Foundations & Technologies

Keeping a favorite recipe on the counter saves opening the cookbook every time. A copy on your own counter, a shared noticeboard and a public display have different space, privacy and update rules. Choose the place and tool after deciding what the copy is allowed to mean.

These five lessons connect caching value and scope to local memory, Redis and Memcached. The product label does not decide whether a value is authoritative, durable, fresh or safe to share.
