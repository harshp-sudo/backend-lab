Read & Write Caching Patterns

A librarian can let you fetch a missing book yourself, fetch it on your behalf, update the catalog before replying, or queue an update for later. The visible result may look similar, but who does the work and when it is considered accepted change the failure story.

Read paths and write acknowledgment boundaries are separate choices. These lessons identify the actual loader/writer, authoritative store and recovery responsibilities instead of promising consistency from a pattern name.
