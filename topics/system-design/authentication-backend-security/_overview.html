Authentication & Backend Security

A club checks who you are at the door, then checks which rooms you may enter. Keys, guest passes and locked filing cabinets protect different things. Good security follows the whole visit, including a lost pass, a broken lock and a change in membership.

This branch connects identity, permission, credential/data protection and safe handling of untrusted input. Start from the actor, resource and threat rather than choosing one token or encryption setting as a universal solution.
