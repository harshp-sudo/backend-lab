Cryptography & Secrets Management

A password checker, a machine key, a locked cabinet and the key office solve different problems. Keeping a secret safe includes creating, using, replacing and retiring it.

Choose the protection for the actual data and attacker boundary, then trace keys/credentials through deployment, logs, backups, rotation and outages.
