Scaling & Traffic Distribution

A busy restaurant can buy a larger oven, open another kitchen, send diners to less busy rooms, or assign neighborhoods to delivery teams. These are different choices: more capacity, traffic assignment and data ownership must fit together.

These three lessons explain scaling direction, load-balancer decisions and consistent key placement. A larger fleet is not automatically faster if every request still waits on the same small resource.
