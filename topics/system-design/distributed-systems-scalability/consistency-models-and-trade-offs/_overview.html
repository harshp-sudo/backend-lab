Consistency Models & Trade-Offs

Two noticeboards show the same schedule. When the messenger is delayed, one board may show yesterday’s plan. You must decide whether an old answer is acceptable or whether readers should wait for a trustworthy answer.

Specify the object, operation and failure before choosing a guarantee. CAP and PACELC frame different trade-offs; eventual and strong consistency describe contracts that need precise assumptions.
