Distributed Transactions & Coordination

A school trip needs seats, permission slips and a driver. Agreeing that every booking will happen, undoing an already-made booking, and choosing who gives instructions are separate problems. A late instruction from yesterday’s organizer can still cause trouble.

This path separates atomic commit, compensating workflows, election and exclusive effects. Each mechanism has its own durable state, uncertainty and recovery obligations.
