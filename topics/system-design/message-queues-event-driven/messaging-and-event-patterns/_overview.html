Messaging & Event Patterns

A school announcement, a daily recorded broadcast, a permanent decision journal and a separate lookup index serve different purposes. Having a record of an announcement does not automatically make it the official history of every decision.

These four lessons distinguish independent subscribers, retained event processing, authoritative event history and separate command/query models. They can be combined when their ownership and recovery contracts fit.
