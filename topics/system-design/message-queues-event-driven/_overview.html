Message Queues & Event-Driven Systems

A school office leaves work slips in named trays. Helpers can pick up work later, different departments can receive their own copy of a notice, and an unfinished slip needs a recovery rule. A tray helps separate people in time; it does not prove that the requested work happened.

This branch follows asynchronous intent from publication through storage, delivery, processing and recovery. Choose a broker and event pattern only after naming what is authoritative, what success means and how much delay, duplication or loss the business can accept.
