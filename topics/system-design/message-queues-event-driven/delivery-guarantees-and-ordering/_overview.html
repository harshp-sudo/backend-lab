Delivery Guarantees & Ordering

A messenger may repeat a delivery when nobody answers, throw away a slip after one attempt, or coordinate a signed receipt with a recorded task. Seeing slips in order is different from finishing their tasks in that order.

Guarantees apply to specific boundaries under explicit failure assumptions. Name publication, delivery, progress acknowledgment, local effects and external outcomes before using once-only language.
