Lifecycle & Production Observability

A shop closing for the night stops inviting new customers, finishes the orders it owns and checks that nobody is left waiting. Its open sign, fire alarm and sales report answer different questions. A service likewise needs separate traffic, recovery and user-outcome signals.

Graceful Shutdown follows owned work through bounded draining. Health Checks controls whether to send traffic, wait for startup or restart. Performance Monitoring connects user-visible behavior to resource and request evidence.
