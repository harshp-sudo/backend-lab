# Editorial Rejection Cases

Reviewed: 2026-11-17. These deliberately weak examples can have valid HTML and still fail. The judgments below are editorial decisions, not claims that prose quality can be established by counting labels.

| Case | Deliberately deficient answer | Required disposition and rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Definition-only | A gateway routes requests. What is a gateway? | FAIL — no concrete production constraint or architectural decision; rejects specificity/production-prompt criteria. |
| Superficial duplicate | Two cards both ask to retry the same payment timeout, changing only the customer name. | FAIL — no materially different failure or decision; rejects distinctness. |
| Missing decision | A partition can be hard; it depends on requirements. | FAIL — no recommended action, owner, guarantee or actionable recovery steps. |
| Missing alternative/cost | Always add services because they scale. | FAIL — no cost, viable alternative or constraint-specific decision. |
| Missing follow-up | Use an outbox; this solves delivery. | FAIL — omits duplicate consumption, changed assumptions and the external-effect boundary. |
| Unsupported frequency | This exact prompt is asked most often at Company X; citation is its product documentation. | FAIL — technical documentation does not establish company interview provenance or frequency. Remove the claim and disclose adaptation, or supply actual appropriate interview evidence. |
| False research limitation | Web search was unavailable, although successful concept queries are recorded. | FAIL — contradicts actual availability; disclose the narrower unverified-frequency gap. |
| Incomplete full delivery | The three constitutional labels exist but the decision paragraph says see the source link. | FAIL — answers must be complete and self-contained; a label/link is not an explanation. |
| Wrong technical guarantee | mTLS means every authenticated service may access every tenant's object. | FAIL — confuses identity/encryption with authorization; verify resource permissions at the owner. |

All nine cases were assessed against the eight criteria in the research/review contract and are rejected. Accepted lesson cards must supply their own concrete prompt, decision, cost/alternative, trap/changed assumption and honest source mapping. A structural pass cannot override these dispositions.
